Privacy policy

gogcli local is a personal command-line tool used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Drive, Calendar and other Google services).

Data obtained through Google APIs is processed only on the owner's computer, is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. OAuth tokens are stored locally in the system keychain and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
